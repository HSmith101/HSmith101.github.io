THE DIVISION ALGORITHM

The big idea

For any integer b and any positive integer a, there are unique integers q and r such that

b = aq + r   with   0 ≤ r < a.

If the divisor is negative, the remainder condition becomes 0 ≤ r < |a|. In this lab, practice divisors are positive unless a question explicitly says otherwise. The remainder is never negative, including when the dividend is negative.

Where this leads

Modular arithmetic

Two integers have the same remainder upon division by m exactly when they are congruent modulo m.

Greatest common divisors

Repeated division produces the gcd as the last nonzero remainder.

Extended Euclidean algorithm

Work backward through the division equations to express the gcd as a combination of the original integers. That's the natural next lab.
