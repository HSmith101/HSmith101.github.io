Two useful rules

Independent events:
P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

Use multiplication when one event does not change the probability of the other.

Complement:
P(not A) = 1 − P(A)

For “at least one,” it's often easier to find the probability of none, then subtract from 1.

Cards: Replacing a card and shuffling makes successive draws independent. Without replacement, the probabilities change.

Assumes fair coins, standard six-sided dice, and a 52-card deck without jokers. Card draws here are always with replacement unless otherwise stated.
