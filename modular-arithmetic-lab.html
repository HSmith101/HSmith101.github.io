Quick reference

Reducing: a ≡ r (mod m) means that m divides a − r. The standard remainder is one of 0, 1, …, m−1 for m > 0.

Arithmetic: you can reduce before adding or multiplying: ab ≡ (a mod m)(b mod m) (mod m).

Solutions: for f(x) ≡ 0 (mod m), test residues from 0 to m−1. A solution residue represents infinitely many integer solutions.

Important: division/cancellation is not always valid modulo m. For ax ≡ b (mod m), a solution exists exactly when gcd(a,m) divides b.
