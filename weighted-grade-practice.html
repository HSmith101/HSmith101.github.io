The big idea

A weighted grade is a sum of category scores multiplied by how much each category counts. The percentages in a complete grading scheme add up to 100%.

Course grade = (weight₁ × score₁) + (weight₂ × score₂) + …

Use weights as decimals: 50% becomes 0.50. For example, the study guide gives 50% participation and in-class work, 30% quizzes, and 20% presentation. If participation is 100%, quizzes are 50%, and the presentation is 100%:

(0.50 × 100) + (0.30 × 50) + (0.20 × 100) = 50 + 15 + 20 = 85%
① Overall grade

Multiply every category's weight by its score, then add the contributions.

② Best possible grade

For categories not yet completed, use 100% if you're asked for the maximum possible grade.

③ Missing or required score

Subtract the known weighted contributions from the target grade, then divide by the missing category's weight.

Scores on individual assignments within a category may need to be averaged first. In this activity, each category percentage is provided directly, so students can focus on weighted percentages.
